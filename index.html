01The Company

A production company built around stories worth telling.

Phosphor Films is an independent film production company based in the American Midwest. Our work centers on character-driven stories — comedies, dramas, and the territory between them.

The name comes from the Greek phosphoros: bearer of light. It's the same root as photography and the language of cinema itself — writing with light.

02Founder

Blair K.
Hartman

Writer · Director · Producer

Blair K. Hartman is a writer, director, and producer based in Indianapolis. He founded Phosphor Films to develop original short and feature work, with a focus on character comedy and stories drawn from the American Midwest.
